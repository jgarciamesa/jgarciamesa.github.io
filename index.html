01 / ABOUT

Connecting research
and computation.

I’m a Research Software Engineer in Arizona State University’s Computational Research Accelerator. I work alongside researchers to build software and make effective use of advanced computing resources.

My work ranges from optimizing applications and migrating software to the supercomputer to helping research teams adopt new technologies, including large language models and retrieval-augmented generation. I also develop and deliver workshops and tutorials around the services we provide.

My background is in computational biology: I earned a Ph.D. in Biological Design at ASU, developing statistical methods and software for sequence alignment. That experience shapes how I approach software—as part of the scientific process.

HPC · COMPUTATIONAL BIOLOGY01

AlphaFold 3 at scale

Adapted an existing AlphaFold 3 pipeline to fit ASU’s HPC architecture and run efficiently at the scale of approximately 20,000 samples.

The workflow pairs a viral protein with human proteome sequences, examining ipTM scores to identify potential candidates for downstream analysis.

SCIENTIFIC SOFTWARE · OPEN SOURCE02

COATi

Built a statistical pairwise aligner for protein-coding DNA sequences, incorporating complex insertion and deletion models, codon evolution, and sequencing base-calling errors.

Developed during my doctoral research, connecting statistical modeling with a C++ implementation and software engineering practices.
